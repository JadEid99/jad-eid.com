# The Island Temple: features

Everything on the page, and where its code lives. Each scene extra is one function in `extras.js`, called from `main.js`. Paths starting `_raw/` or `_tools/` are in the local workspace with the source assets, not in this repo.

## Features

| Feature | What it does | Code | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sky` | Golden-hour gradient sky with a soft sun glow. Also lights the scene (environment map, strong enough to light the shade, e.g. the lower island under the hand), moves the sun low for long shadows, and darkens behind the page text for legibility. | `sky()` · colours in `SKY_COLORS`, sun angle in `SUN` | The other features look best with it on. |
| `clouds` | A bed of cloud below the island and a distant ring behind it, drifting slowly. Clouds near the camera fade out, so the low staircase views stay clear. | `clouds()` | Generated in code; no image files. |
| `ao` | Ambient occlusion: darkens creases and contact points (column bases, fingers, carving). | `ambientOcclusion()` | Costs GPU time; first to cut if the page feels slow. |
| `marble` | Polished marble: faint clear coat plus a soft sheen on columns, ring and statues. | `polishMarble()` | |
| `detail` | Fine surface detail on rock, hand, steps and ground, so close-ups stay crisp. | `detailTextures()` | Re-uses each material's own maps at 9× tiling. |
| `water` | Reflective, rippling pool in the temple floor. | `water()` | Ripple texture loads from the three.js GitHub mirror on jsDelivr. |
| `waterfall` | Modelled on tall free falls (Staubbachfall, Bridalveil Fall): water springs from an arched outlet pressed into the platform's wall beneath a column (Blender; the top surface is untouched, so the column keeps solid ground), falls as a sunlit column of water with real thickness (an elliptical cross-section that widens and deepens as it falls, so it holds up from the side), plus a glinting outer shell and two strands drifting apart; its flow speeds up and breaks into lace, carries falling "comet" clumps with trailing streaks, sprays at the lip, and dissolves into a sunlit mist plume above the clouds. Lit by the scene's sun, including the glow when the sun shines through it. | `waterfall()` · `tubeGeo()`, `sheetMaterial()`, `cometMaterial()` | Shape and position in `WF`. The outlet is real geometry: `_tools/blender/carve_spillway.py` edits the platform mesh (`_raw/temple_mod/model_1.obj`), which `build-temple.mjs` picks up. Keep `WF.angle` in sync with `A` in that script. |
| `sound` | Waterfall ambience, off until the visitor clicks the speaker in the nav (browsers block autoplay). Looped seamlessly in the browser, louder as the camera nears the fall. | `waterfallSound()` · `assets/audio/waterfall.m4a` | “Catarata Paqchaq” by Yupanasimi, CC BY-SA 4.0. AAC, 0.4 MB (FLAC original in `_raw/audio/originals`), loaded only on first click. |
| `music` | Background music (`sirens.m4a`, ~2:39), looped seamlessly by crossfading its last 4 s into its start; plays at a steady level under the waterfall. Shares the speaker button with `sound`. | `music()` · `addTrack()` · `assets/audio/sirens.m4a` | Level in `MUSIC_VOLUME`. |
| `muffle` | The Sirens episode's "wax in the ears": all audio runs through a low-pass filter. With the book hero it follows the way through the page: fully muffled (~380 Hz) at the book, clearing step by step up the stairs, clear at the temple, and muffled again as the view pulls away for Contact. Without the book it goes by the camera's distance to the temple. | `muffle()` · values in `MUFFLE` | `far`/`near` (no book) are camera distances to the temple in metres. |
| `inscriptions` | Each discipline's Latin name (Cloud & Security = NUBES, Platforms & DevOps = FUNDAMENTA, Software Engineering = OFFICINA, AI & Computer Vision = RATIO, Robotics & Automation = AUTOMATA, Leadership & Delivery = CONSILIUM) cut into the ring's own marble (in the shader): darkened recesses with bevelled edges that catch the light, gilding in the grooves. The active column's name glows. | `inscriptions()` · `epigraph()` | English and Latin live together in `PILLARS` (`main.js`): `name` for the legend, `latin` for the carving, written in normal spelling and converted to inscription form (capitals, · between words; U and J kept as written). Cinzel font; lettering height via `BAND`. |
| `book` | The hero: visitors start close on a closed, worn leather book (burnished edges and corners, scuffs, rubbed antique gilding, rough deckled page edges) on a weathered lectern near the lower island's edge, on the right as seen in Contact, facing out from the island; the reader stands on the island side and looks out over the book. Name and current role are gold-tooled into the cover (pressed into the leather and gilded, worn in places) ("Scroll to open" below). The first scroll opens it: the cover swings over, then three pages turn, curling and casting their shadow on the page below. They carry Homer's Odyssey in the Greek (a title page, the opening lines, the Cyclops "Nobody", the Sirens' song) on aged parchment (stains, foxing, nicked edges) and end on a spread with a few words on what the site is, the current role ("Presently") and LinkedIn, CV and GitHub icons on the left, and the five certifications with their badges and a link icon on the right. Every certificate and icon is a link (hover highlights it). Scrolling on, the camera pulls back and takes the short way round to the front of the stairs, into Work. Scrolling back to the top closes the book. The HTML hero (with the same links) stays in the page for screen readers. | `heroBook()` · text in `ABOUT`, `PRESENTLY`, `COVER`, `CERTS`, `PROFILES`, `ODYSSEY` · sizes, spot (`bearing`, `inset`) and timing in `BOOK` · lectern weathering in `weather()` | Lectern: black marble with gold (`assets/lectern-black.glb`, prepared by `_tools/blender/lecterns.py`; the black podium's top is hollow, so a lid is laid across it in code, on a plane fitted through its sloping wall tops, and the lectern is turned so it slopes down towards the reader). It rests on the lowest ground under its base. No light sources of its own: the sky lights it (see `sky`), plus a faint reading light on the pages. Leather: Poly Haven "Brown Leather" (CC0), worn in code; parchment generated in code; Greek set in GFS Didot. |
| `staircase` | The career as a climb, to scale: each chapter (carved B.Eng, Education, Robotics I (BMW), M.Sc, Robotics II (sewts), Software (Telekom), Ventures (Evolve & CloudChamp), Cloud (AWS)) is carved into the step matching its start date, so the steps between chapters show how long each lasted (MMXX · B.ENG on the bottom step, today a few steps below the top). The M.Sc is listed before sewts and both show 2022 (sewts started 11/2021), so the degree reads as finished before the job. The top steps are left uncut, with PLUS · ULTRA ("further beyond") carved among them for "What comes next", the last timeline entry. Roman capitals carved into the stone itself (no gold): a deep, even shadow in the cut whose walls catch the light, with chipped edges (`STEP.wear`), in a dressed face (the same stone with its busy detail smoothed, `STEP.dress`) that fades out towards the riser's edges (no border); the lettering follows each riser's own lean and sideways tilt and stays inside its corners and above the ground. In Work the camera climbs to each chapter's step as its entry in the timeline beside it comes into view; that step's engraving catches fire (`fire`; a warm glow without it) and the stone around it darkens a little (`STEP.panel`). The page's dark scrim swaps to the text side in Work, so the steps aren't dimmed. | `staircase()` · data in `MILESTONES` (`year` + `month` = start, `to` = end, `carve` = text cut into the step; an entry without a date is carved among the uncut steps) · look and `future` (uncut steps) in `STEP` | Today is the real current date, so the scale updates over time; the order of `MILESTONES` is kept, and a chapter whose step is taken moves up to the next free one. Steps are found in the mesh: each riser is a quad, grouped along the stairs (`STEP.gap`); its corners give the lettering's axes. Lettering width `MARGIN` keeps clear of the rock beside the stairs. Scrim swap: `body.at-work` in `temple.css`. |
| `fire` | The highlighted step's engraving burns, inside the grooves only: a dark red bed with thin tongues of flame rising and flickering through each stroke, brightest where the stroke is deepest; the stone beneath goes dark and the firelight on the step flickers faintly. Fades in and out with the highlight. | Fire block in the `staircase()` shader (`STEP_FIRE`) | Needs `staircase`. Drawn in the step's own material: no extra objects. |
| `credentials` | CV/LinkedIn/GitHub in the nav and the languages line in Contact (the certificates and "Presently" are in the book). | `index.html` | CV links to the Google Drive copy (same link as the original site), which is kept up to date. |

## Page

- **Scroll cue**: a pill at the bottom of the screen all the way down ("Scroll to open" on the closed book, "Scroll" after), hidden at the very end; clicking it moves on by most of a screen. In `main.js` (`updateCue`) and `temple.css`.
- **Name**: JAD in the corner, scrambling into Τζαντ and back every 10 s (`main.js`).

- **Expertise, one pillar at a time**: scrolling walks the camera round the colonnade; the column facing it lights up (with its carved name), its numeral in the I–VI index is marked, and a panel (laid out like a Work entry) shows that pillar's skills (`showPillar()` in `main.js`; pillars and their skills in `PILLARS`, `main.js`). Scroll is the only control: no hover, no letter keys.
- **Hero text**: the HTML hero (name, role, tagline, "Presently", certification links) is kept for screen readers only (`sr-only`); visitors see it in the book.
- **Contact**: heading, the email link and the languages line.

## Devices and loading

- **One model for everyone**: `temple.glb`, 1-2K WebP maps, 6.3 MB, about 220 MB of GPU memory. The 4K build is in `_raw/retired/`. KTX2 was tried (`_tools/ktx2.mjs`): darker, blurrier textures and no smaller download, so not used.
- **Loading screen**: covers everything until the model, the book, the stairs, fonts and every shader are ready and a first frame is drawn; no scrolling meanwhile. The visit always starts at the book (or at a linked section, e.g. `#contact`).
- **Audio**: AAC (`.m4a`), 2 MB in all (originals in `_raw/audio/originals`); loads only when sound is switched on.
- **Lighter render** (`lowPower` in `shared/base.js`: phones, tablets, 4 cores or fewer, 4 GB or less): sharpness at most 1.5x, no ambient-occlusion pass, smaller sun shadow. On every device, if frames stay under about 40 a second, the render drops resolution in steps (down to 0.75x); it never steps back up.
- **Phones upright** (`portrait()`, under 800 px wide): the scene sits in the upper part of the screen, the text in the lower part on a dark fade; the open book shows one page at a time and pans from the left page to the right as you scroll; the links fold into a Menu (the sound button stays in the header). The canvas is sized to the tallest the viewport gets, so the address bar sliding doesn't jolt the view.
- **Phones sideways** (under 500 px tall): desktop layout with smaller type, the Menu, and a two-column skills panel.
